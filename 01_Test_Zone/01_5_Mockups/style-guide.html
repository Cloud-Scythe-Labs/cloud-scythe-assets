06 / BRAND MARKS

EMBLEMS, LOGOTYPES, LOCKUPS

Shipped SVGs, filled with --fg through a mask. Only the Duo2 ring takes color, always with a status label. Duo marks sit on text backgrounds only.

07 / PHOTOGRAPHY

PRODUCT PHOTOGRAPHY

Photos sit on the page with --radius corners and nothing else: no border, CSS filter, glow or overlay. Treatment lives in the file.

DIRECTION

  • Black and white, high contrast.
  • Light backdrops; the subject reads dark against them.
  • Subtle film grain, baked into the file.
  • Skin tones may stay warm: the only color allowed.
  • No filters, glows, overlays or gradients added in CSS.

08 / MOTION

MOTION

Three moves, all decorative or attention-only: the Duo2 ring cycle, emblem distortion, and the ticker. Nothing moves under reduced motion.

DUO2 COLOR CYCLE

Ring steps red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink, idle; 0.5 s each. Loading and attention only, never a status. Reduced motion rests on idle gray.

EMBLEM DISTORTION

Designer v4 warps the emblem as a motion effect. Allowed only in hero, promo and loading moments, always animated back to the true mark.

Never on the favicon, app icon, print or any static use; there §4.2 "never warp" holds.

TICKER

Divider below: alternating rows rotated 30°, looping every 5.67 s, in --fg-on-primary on the page fill. Carries no text you need to read; stills under reduced motion.
