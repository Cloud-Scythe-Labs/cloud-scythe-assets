NOCTURNE

Cold light in a black room. The marks and typefaces stay; colour, surfaces, layout and motion change. One accent, muted status, hairlines instead of boxes, and motion that snaps in and drifts out.

PROPOSED DIRECTION THEME PS2 07 SECTIONS ↓

01 DIRECTION

FIVE WORDS. EACH ONE IS A RULE.

D.01

FAST

Speed reads in straight lines and instant response.

RULE--snap easing, 120 ms response, 2 px corners, speed-sweep hover, rotated ticker.

D.02

DARK

The PS2 boot black: the page is a void, light is the exception.

RULE--void page, cold near-black bands, grayscale imagery that fades into the void.

D.03

INTIMIDATING

Scale and restraint: huge titles, few words, nothing friendly.

RULEOne-line Display up to 176 px, HUD labels over chatty copy, no pills, no rounded cards.

D.04

ETHEREAL

Cold light in fog: glow replaces fill, shapes materialise.

RULEOne accent (--ether) for focus and glow, fog atmosphere, blur-to-sharp reveals, status as a lit pixel.

D.05

EXCITING

Motion and contrast spikes: the one bright thing matters.

RULETicker bands between sections, primary action in solid --fg, live pulse, 60 ms reveal stagger.
