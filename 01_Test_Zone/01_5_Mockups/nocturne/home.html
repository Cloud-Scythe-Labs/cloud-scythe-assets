03 REACLOUD PIPELINE

FROM THE ARRANGE VIEW TO EVERY MACHINE IN SECONDS

  1. 01 RECORD

    The extension watches your project as you work. Nothing to export.

    $ reacloud status
night_drive.rpp   3 changed

  2. 02 COMMIT

    Snapshot the session with a message. Every version stays recoverable.

    $ reacloud commit -m "tighten drums"
7c1e9a4  tighten drums

  3. 03 PUSH

    The daemon uploads in the background and collaborators pull it.

    $ reacloud push
pushed  142 MB  ok
