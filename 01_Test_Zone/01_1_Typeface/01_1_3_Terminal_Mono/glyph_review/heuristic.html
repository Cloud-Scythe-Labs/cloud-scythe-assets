What I took from your 124 answers: keep JetBrains Mono's letterforms (103 kept), and bring over two CSL qualities everywhere, letters and digits first, symbols in a later pass:

  • Even stroke width. CSL strokes are the same width all the way round; JetBrains Mono tapers where curves meet stems (top of a, foot of 9, curve of 2, the rings of %) and draws horizontals thinner than verticals (e, i, M).
  • Squarer curvature. CSL's curve starts later, so straight sides run longer and the corner is tighter, while staying a real curve (not the "Comic Sans" over-tight version).
  • Specific ports: ? and ¿ take CSL's shape (bigger dot, fitted to JetBrains Mono's width and height, ¿ lowered); Q gets a vertical tail; 0's slash takes position and weight from the CSL branding font; i keeps JetBrains Mono's dot; 6 and 9 keep JetBrains Mono's open forms so they can't be read as b / g.
